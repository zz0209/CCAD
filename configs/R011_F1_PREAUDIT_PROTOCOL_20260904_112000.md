# R011-F1 pre-audit protocol v1 — Fuzzy Contribution Correspondence

Status: **FROZEN FOR IMPLEMENTATION; REAL CALIBRATION NOT YET READ**

Parent: `R011-F1` / C040 / C044

Evidence target: bounded real-SAE discovery and calibration feasibility only. This protocol cannot itself establish C1-FCC or C2-FCC.

## 1. Question and independent unit

For a target-blind, source-only atom query, can a paired low-rank relation between local native contribution banks distinguish the query process from energy-matched source-side hard negatives and from global/raw relations, while preserving overlap and refusing non-identifiable competing relations?

The independent descriptive unit is `source query × ordered source-target seed pair`. Tokens are weighted repeated measurements inside that unit, never independent replicates. Seed pair, query and energy stratum remain explicit in all summaries.

## 2. Fixed data and information boundary

- Base assets: R008b five-seed paired sparse codes at Pythia-160M layer 5, width 3072, TopK k128.
- Query frame: the immutable R009b 640-query panel; the first feasibility screen uses exactly the lowest existing selection hash in each `source seed × 8 energy strata` block (40 queries). It is a bounded gate, not a replacement for the full panel.
- Mean constants: per-feature code means from R009a's independent `mean` split.
- Relation and C040 metric fitting: `discovery` only.
- Calibration: evaluates discovery-frozen candidates/loadings and selects only rank, numerical thresholds and refusal cutoffs.
- Audit: forbidden and unopened. No audit tensor, metric, candidate, endpoint or threshold may be read by an R011-F1 discovery/calibration process.

If the 40-query screen passes, the same frozen implementation and numerical rules are expanded once to all 640 queries before any audit. A failed screen ends this protocol version; it does not authorize another candidate or threshold variant.

## 3. Query weights and candidate universes

For source atom `i`, positive discovery weights are proportional to squared source code and restricted to the 256 highest-energy discovery tokens. The same deterministic rule is used on mean and calibration when evaluating support/effect floors. A query is unevaluable if it has fewer than 8 document-respecting effective samples or fewer than 8 active discovery sequences / 4 mean or calibration sequences.

Hard-negative weights are constructed source-only. Within the same source seed and R009b energy stratum, take the next three query atoms in immutable selection-hash order, excluding the positive query; pool their squared-code weights and retain the top 256 tokens. Target codes or outcomes never choose hard negatives.

For each ordered seed pair:

- source family: the query atom plus 31 source atoms with highest absolute positive-weight centered code covariance with it, ties by atom ID;
- target family: deterministic deduplicated union of the R009c top-32 balanced-BCC, direct-contribution, decoder-cosine and Hungarian witness banks, ordered by first method/rank appearance and capped at 128;
- no family is enlarged from calibration results; candidate counts and all deduplication steps are logged;
- every baseline receives this same source/target universe, rank family and feature-pair evaluation budget.

This family is a proposal surface, not a completeness certificate. Failure is `UNRESOLVED_RELATION`, never absence.

## 4. Sparse contribution kernel and FCC object

For independently centered codes, kernels are computed without materializing a `[token, feature, hook]` tensor:

\[
G^s_{ij}=\mathbb E_w[(z_i^s-\mu_i^s)(z_j^s-\mu_j^s)]\langle d_i^s,Md_j^s\rangle,
\]

with analogous target and cross kernels. The implementation must match explicit dense contributions on a deterministic conformance fixture.

Whitened paired CCA yields loadings `(A_q,B_q)`, canonical values and the relation operator `K_q=A_q diag(sigma_q) B_q^T`. Only `K_q`, its normalized absolute coupling, marginals and effective supports are treated as the correspondence object. Factor coordinates are gauge-dependent and cannot be interpreted as canonical concepts.

Candidate ranks are exactly `{1,2,4,8}`. Euclidean FCC is always reported. C040 replaces the Euclidean hook inner product by a discovery-frozen PSD downstream-sensitivity pullback; it does not change queries or candidates.

## 5. C040 metric freeze

The C040 metric is query-agnostic and fitted before any FCC calibration read:

- 256 discovery hook states selected by a fixed content hash, balanced across documents;
- four deterministic Rademacher hook directions per state, with central `+/-` perturbations at `0.01 × discovery hook RMS`;
- output is a fixed 256-dimensional signed sketch of next-token logits; sketch seed and vocabulary projection are stored;
- ridge fraction `1e-4`, eigenvalue relative tolerance `1e-6`, trace normalization as implemented in `fit_probe_metric`;
- the causal metric, probe ledger, model/hook revision and all hashes are frozen as a separate artifact before relation fitting.

Because this metric uses next-token logits, next-state residual is the primary independent causal screen endpoint. Logit effects are secondary and cannot be the sole causal validation of the metric that used a logit sketch.

## 6. Contrast, collision and ambiguity

For each rank and metric, report positive canonical values, discovery-frozen loadings, hard-negative cross energy, positive-minus-negative contrast, coupling entropy/effective support, rank-boundary relative gap, and transfer BCC/residual on calibration.

Cross-query collision is the Bhattacharyya overlap of target marginals for queries in the same source-seed/target-seed and energy stratum. True structural overlap is allowed: overlap alone is not failure. Failure is a lack of query contrast—high collision together with no positive-vs-hard-negative advantage—or equality with the raw/global control.

`UNRESOLVED_RELATION` reason codes are orthogonal and may co-occur:

- `SOURCE_CONDITION_INSUFFICIENT`;
- `CANDIDATE_BUDGET_REFUSAL`;
- `NUMERICAL_RANK_BELOW_REQUESTED`;
- `COMPETING_RELATION_RANK_BOUNDARY`;
- `HARD_NEGATIVE_CONTRAST_NOT_POSITIVE`;
- `CROSS_QUERY_COLLISION_NOT_BETTER_THAN_GLOBAL`;
- `CALIBRATION_TRANSFER_GATE_FAILED`;
- `CAUSAL_SPECIFICITY_GATE_FAILED`.

The rank-boundary and threshold cutoffs are selected only on calibration from the fixed family. No relation is forced when the best and next rank boundary are indistinguishable.

## 7. Matched controls and budget

Mandatory controls are Euclidean FCC, raw-hook/global relation, sparsity-controlled or semantic-free OT, Li15 spectral many-to-many, relaxed stitching/MAS-style alignment, SCT marginal projectors, native/MSCC, best functional single and matched random. Semantic labels are not used in this first protocol.

All relation methods share candidate IDs, ranks, positive/hard-negative token ledgers and at most `32 × 128 = 4096` feature-pair entries per query-pair. Controls that do not naturally emit a coupling must still report matched rank, energy, coverage and selection count. Runtime is descriptive, not a winner-selection criterion.

## 8. Progression and stopping rule

Calibration may freeze a minimum acceptable rank and thresholds, but may not refit C040, queries, candidates or hard negatives. The 40-query screen progresses only if all are true:

1. evaluable FCC coverage is at least 10% overall, covers at least four of eight energy strata and is nonzero in every ordered seed direction represented by an evaluable query;
2. positive-minus-hard-negative contrast is positive for at least 75% of proposed `FOUND_RELATION` units;
3. median cross-query collision is lower than the matched raw/global relation by at least 0.05 absolute;
4. on the frozen bounded next-state causal screen, FCC has positive query-specificity or effect-consistency gain over both global/raw and Euclidean FCC, with the primary comparison matched for rank and perturbation RMS.

If any condition fails, the result is a bounded pre-audit negative (`UNRESOLVED_RELATION`) and R012/R013 remain closed. A pass only authorizes one full-640 pre-audit freeze; it is not C1/C2-FCC evidence. Audit opens only after the full panel, all baselines, thresholds, means, statistics and endpoint code hashes are frozen.
